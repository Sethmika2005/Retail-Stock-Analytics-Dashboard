Problem Domain

Investors have access to numbers and price charts, but no
system that turns those numbers into clear, justified
guidance.
This is the reality for over 150 million retail investors today.

Cost Barrier: Professional terminals like
Bloomberg/Refinitiv cost $20,000+/year.

Raw Data: Access to charts & ratios ≠ unified
interpretation.

Knowledge Gap: Understanding metrics like
MACD or RSI requires expertise most users lack.

“The gap isn’t access to data –
its interpretation.”

Academic Signals to Actionable Intelligence
(Literature Review)

Existing Literature

EMA Crossover – Trend Detection
When EMA20 crosses above the EMA50,
it’s a potential uptrend.

Volume Confirmation — Validating the Signal
When the Average Traded Volume slope is
rising during a crossover, it confirms
institutional participation.

RSI Safety Gate — Preventing Bad Entries
RSI acts as a guardrail: it blocks buy signals
and sell signals.

Kadia et al. (2025) demonstrated that
combining these three indicators with PPO
reinforcement learning achieves 85%
signal accuracy. But the model is rigid — it
applies the same logic regardless of market
conditions.

Methodology: A Three-layer Intelligence
system

Layer 1: Data & Indicator Computation
Extracts live data and calculates
technical indicators, fundamental
scores, news and detects market
regime and volatility.

Layer 2: Signal Generation
Framework
Transforms indicators into
structured buy, sell, or hold
signals.

Layer 3: Reinforcement
Learning Optimisation
The agent learns optimal
decisions by maximising long-
term returns.

Business Value: Bridging the Gap

Informed, Not Automated
We do not make decisions for investors. We empower them
with structured, data-driven frameworks, translating complex
analysis into plain language so the user retains control.

Premium Data, Democratised
Access to insights previously locked behind $20,000+
professional terminals. We bring institutional-grade data to the
public market—entirely for free.

A Level Playing Field
Historically, retail investors faced a massive information
disadvantage. We eliminate this gap by arming everyday investors
with the same analytical depth used by major institutions.

Learning the “Why”
Users don’t just follow blind signals. Every recommendation
includes the rationale, teaching users how markets behave over
time for long-term financial mastery.
